title: "Dynamic Relational Cults"
date: 2026-11-23
---

One particularly disturbing manifestation of relational coercion 
involves the instrumentalization of sexual relationships with third parties.

Consider a situation in which an individual has developed an emotional attachment to another person. 
The attachment may be intense, one-sided, unresolved or otherwise complicated. 
The other person is aware of its significance. 
Sexual relationships with third parties or implications of it can then become a means of inflicting psychological torture, 
particularly when those third parties carry specific interpersonal significance for the attached individual.

The identities of these third parties matter. They may be people the individual admires, envies, dislikes, distrusts or even despises. 
Their involvement can introduce additional dimensions of humiliation, rivalry, displacement and degradation.

The mechanism is not simply sexual infidelity or the exercise of sexual autonomy. 
It is the alleged deliberate instrumentalization of sexual relationships as a means of exploiting an existing emotional attachment.

What is being weaponized is the meaning of the relationship, the individual's emotional investment, 
and the knowledge of what particular interpersonal configurations represent to them.

In this context, sexual triangulation can become a means of relational torment. 
An intimate attachment is transformed into a point through which another person's dignity and emotional stability can be attacked.

The significance of this phenomenon within dynamic relational cults is that an otherwise private sexual relationship can acquire a wider social dimension. 
Its meanings can intersect with existing friendships, rivalries, acquaintances and social relationships,
potentially making the individual's wider interpersonal environment part of the experience of humiliation.

The object of the coercion, where deliberate coercion is present, is not necessarily to obtain sexual compliance.
It is to exploit attachment itself: to turn emotional investment into vulnerability and vulnerability into 
an instrument of degradation and perhapse group conformance.
