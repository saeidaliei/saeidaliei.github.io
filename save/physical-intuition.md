<a id="one"></a> ### 1. Why gravity always pulls, but electromagnetism can push or pull?


Electromagnetism is a *spin-1* interaction. Charge comes in two signs, and exchanging a spin-1 photon between same-sign charges produces repulsion, while opposite signs produce attraction -- a general feature of odd-spin force carriers in quantum field theory. Gravity is a spin-2 interacrion and for even-spin exchange math works the opposite way: the force between the two sources of the same sign is always attractive, regardless of what that sign is. The other half: gravity's source, mass-energy, has only ever been observed to be positive.
